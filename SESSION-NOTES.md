## Decisions made

**Recruiters are the primary channel.** Last three jobs came through recruiters. The
strategy initially underweighted this; corrected. Direct applications continue in
parallel but at lower expected conversion.

**Two tracks, both valid.** Editorial-led (Deepgram archetype) and data-driven
full-funnel (6sense archetype). Not a hedge — the Mendix arc plus Bolt.new covers both.

**B2C is not a hard no.** The screen is whether a company has *decided* who it's
talking to, not whether consumers are in the mix.

**Volume target: 2–4 applications/week.** Only roles clearing the seven-criteria bar.
Consequence: sourcing is the bottleneck, not applying.

**Company-site applications only.** Aggregators are discovery. Superpath and Ashby
search identified as highest-yield.

**Resume structure fixed.** Dates corrected (Iron Mountain July 2015–Aug 2019, Mendix
Sept 2019–Feb 2026), Bolt.new added, each Mendix title given its own range, Internet
Brands restored.

**"Why looking so soon" reframed.** From passive ("the role turned out to be a muddy
mix") to active ("the audience picture was unclear, so I did the research, defined the
segments, and built a customer story pipeline"). Same facts, better position.

**Passed on Cloudinary.** JD matched well; Glassdoor flagged leadership churn, no clear
brand identity, constant plan changes, short-notice RTO.

## Market context

2026 tech layoffs running ahead of 2025 — roughly 172K workers across ~497 events,
about 810/day. Content creation repeatedly named among functions absorbed during AI
reorganizations (Monday.com −20%, Oracle −13%, Cloudflare −20%, all AI-framed).

This cuts both ways. It's a risk in any role where content is production capacity, and
an argument for the roles being targeted, where content is strategy and program
leadership. The AI tool stack work is the strongest answer to "couldn't AI do this" —
but it has to be *visible*, not just true.

## Known constraint

Self-promotion is uncomfortable. Mitigations built into the strategy:
- All four editorial pillars are subject-first. Writing about AI tooling or GEO/AEO
  measurement is doing the work in public, not promoting the person.
- Recruiter conversations are the deliberate exception — they need the pitch handed to
  them directly, and modesty there is counterproductive.
