Most content leaders talk about AI fluency. Fewer have built the tooling their team runs on.

**The build.** Three tools on one design system. ContentedCal holds the plan, a React and
Supabase calendar running 417 content items across 13 active projects. write-strike does the
writing, 11 skills shipped to the team through a private plugin marketplace. Copydesk is where
the writing gets read and edited, an Electron app that picks up a draft the second it exists.

**Why it works.** They pass work to each other through plain files and one database, so there's
no integration to maintain and nothing lost in transit. Every piece moves through all three
without being re-entered once.

**The part that matters.** I built all three with an AI coding agent. I'm a writer, not an
engineer, and that's the point rather than the caveat: the work is the specification, the
design, the interface copy, and the judgment about what a tool should refuse to do.
