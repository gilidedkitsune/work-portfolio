Before a content team can scale, it needs a shared definition of how the brand actually
sounds — one specific enough that ten different writers produce work that reads as one
voice.

**The approach.** I established the Mendix brand voice and editorial guidelines across
blogs, ebooks, white papers, guides, webinars, video, events, and email. I reviewed
content performance data and built the process for asset management and review that
went with it, then used both to drive a cross-functional content program tied to demand
generation.

**The result.** The guidelines became the reference point for every piece of content
Mendix shipped over the next several years — including for the nine-person team built on
top of them.
