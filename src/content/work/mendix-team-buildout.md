A content function of one doesn't scale by hiring alone — it scales by building the
infrastructure that lets a growing team move in the same direction.

**The approach.** I expanded the Mendix content team from 1 to 9 writers and content
strategists in nine months, and built the editorial calendar and process that scaled
alongside the headcount. I developed a full-funnel content program that translated
product marketing positioning into narratives for multiple audiences, and built four new
SEO-optimized, pain-point-focused content BOMs.

**The result.** The new content infrastructure recovered a 22% year-over-year web traffic
deficit, and the team — and the process behind it — became the foundation for the
content organization Mendix ran on through the following four years.
