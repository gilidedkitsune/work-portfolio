As AI answer engines became a real discovery surface, generic SEO stopped being enough — the
question shifted from "do we rank" to "do we get cited."

**The approach.** I diagnosed Bolt.new's AI-answer visibility and citation performance, then
built and executed a GEO/AEO content strategy against the specific gaps that turned up.

**The result.** Own-domain citations grew from 786 to 1,809 in a single month — moving Bolt.new
from #9 to #5 among all cited domains, the largest gain in the top 25 — while holding #1 in
AI-answer visibility in the "AI development tools" category at 57.3%, up 3.6 points
month-over-month.
