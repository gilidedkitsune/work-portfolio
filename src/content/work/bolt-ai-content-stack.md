Most content teams talk about AI fluency. Few have actually built and shipped the tooling
their team runs on — and fewer still have distributed it beyond their own team.

**The approach.** I built an AI-powered tool stack covering the full content lifecycle:
strategy, copywriting, editing, and research. Alongside it, I designed a custom editorial
calendar with Google SSO and direct integrations to Claude, Ordinal, Linear, and Notion —
replacing ad hoc tracking with a system the whole team, and Bolt's wider creator and agency
network, could actually run on.

**The result.** The stack didn't stay internal — it went company-wide and out into the
creator and agency network Bolt works with, alongside a custom dashboard for tracking PR
metrics and GEO/AEO performance. It's the differentiator most content-leadership candidates
can only claim secondhand: I built the tooling, not just adopted it.
