Mendix needed a PR program that could prove its worth in pipeline terms, not just clip
counts — and one that could run consistently across a genuinely global footprint.

**The approach.** I built and ran a data-driven PR program spanning five agencies across
the US, UK, Benelux, DACH, and Singapore, aligning content and PR metrics directly to
marketing goals and company KPIs. I optimized a $1M annual budget, reallocating spend
toward the initiatives that were actually moving coverage and pipeline.

The centerpiece was an integrated campaign built on an original global survey — planned
and executed in four months.

**The result.** Earned media grew to 60% of total coverage in tier-1 publications, a 40%
year-over-year increase. The global survey campaign lifted coverage 75% over previous
surveys within three weeks of launch and influenced $10M in pipeline.
