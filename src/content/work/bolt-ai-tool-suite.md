Most content leaders talk about AI fluency. Fewer have actually built and distributed the
tooling their team runs on.

**The approach.** I built and rolled out a content and writing suite of AI tools, distributed
company-wide. Alongside it, I built a Claude-linked copy desk for editorial review, plus a
custom content calendar covering both content and PR — replacing scattered tracking with
infrastructure the whole team could run on.

**The result.** The tooling didn't stay theoretical — it's the system the team uses daily,
and it's part of the same foundational push that included setting content strategy across
B2B and B2C messaging, launching a new blog, and increasing publication cadence from
1–2 posts a month to 3–4 a week.
